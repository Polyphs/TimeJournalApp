STANDING ON SHOULDERS

Time logs aren't new, and neither is the lesson behind them. Different authors, different books, one thread: review. Look back at where your time went, then decide what comes next. Without that review, effort becomes an endless loop with no goal. timejournal turns that first step into an app.

  • Peter Drucker, The Effective Executive (1967)
  • Stephen R. Covey, A. Roger Merrill and Rebecca R. Merrill, First Things First (1994)
  • David Allen, Getting Things Done (2001)
  • Laura Vanderkam, 168 Hours (2010)
  • Marshall Goldsmith and Mark Reiter, Triggers (2015)
