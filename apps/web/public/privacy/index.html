Privacy

Draft. Review before launch.

What we collect

  • Your email, if you follow the blog or create an account.
  • Your time records, only if you sign in and turn on sync. Without an account, your records stay on your device.

Why

  • To send a welcome note and occasional updates, which you can stop at any time.
  • To sync your records between your devices when you sign in.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your records to train AI.
  • We do not run ads.

Who handles it

This website runs on Netlify. Accounts and synced records are stored with Supabase. Email delivery will use a mail provider that we will name here before we send the first message. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Your choices

Ask us to delete your email or your account and we will. Every email we send will include an unsubscribe link.
